The shift

One workflow. Not forty replies.

Forward, Mon — "resending details, lost the original"

New thread, Sun — same claim, different subject line
Direct message — "any update on parts??"
How a claim moves

Six stages. Always visible, to everyone who needs to see them.

01
Submitted
Sub-dealer enters VIN, mileage, complaint, cause, correction, parts, labor, and photos.
02
Reviewed
Dealer approves with a work order number, returns with a note, or rejects.
03
Awaiting Parts
Parts team tracks each part — ICT, Shipped, VOR, or Cancelled — with tracking number and ETA.
04
Parts Arrived
Sub-dealer confirms every part has physically reached their branch.
05
Repair Submitted
Sub-dealer uploads proof the repair is complete.
06
Closed
Dealer signs off. The claim stays searchable in history, permanently.
Built for every seat

Three roles. One shared source of truth.

Sub-Dealer

Submit & track

  • Submit a claim from any device, no paperwork
  • Watch its status change live — no need to ask
  • Edit and resubmit instantly if something's returned
  • See exactly which parts have shipped
Dealer

Review & control

  • Approve, return, or reject — always with a note
  • Assign the dealer work order number on approval
  • Create every login — full control over who has access
  • See every branch, every claim, in one view
Parts Team

Ship & resolve

  • One queue, sorted oldest-first, by status
  • Set tracking number and ETA per part
  • Nothing to chase down by phone
  • See only what's actually approved
