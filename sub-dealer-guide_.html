QUICK START GUIDE

How to use
WarrantyDesk.

Everything you need to submit and track your warranty claims — start to finish, in about five minutes.

1. Log In2. Submit a Claim3. Track Status 4. Fix & Resubmit5. Parts6. After Repair7. History
STEP 1

Log in with the account we set up for you.

01

Open the link we sent you and go to the login page.

02

Enter the email and password we shared with you separately. (Not the same email as this guide — check for a separate message with your credentials.)

03

You'll land on your dashboard — this is your home screen, showing every claim from your branch.

Didn't get your login? Contact us directly and we'll resend it — don't try to create your own account, the site doesn't allow open sign-up anymore.

STEP 2

Click "New Claim" and fill in the details.

VIN, Mileage, Plate NumberRequired

Work Order NumberRequired — just type the number, your branch code is added automatically

Reception DateRequired

Customer Complaint, Cause of Defect, CorrectionRequired

LaborRequired — code and name, at least one line

PartsOptional — skip this if it's a labor-only claim

AttachmentsRequired — photos or videos of the issue

CommentOptional

Click a photo or video after attaching it to preview it before you submit — and use the × to remove anything attached by mistake.

STEP 3

Watch your claim's status update live.

Tap any status below to see what it means.

STEP 4

If it's returned, fix it and resubmit.

01

Open the claim — you'll see the dealer's note explaining exactly what needs fixing.

02

Edit any field, add or remove parts and labor, and swap out evidence photos as needed.

03

Click "Save & Resubmit Claim." It goes straight back to the dealer for another review.

Nothing is lost when a claim is returned — every field, part, and photo stays exactly as you left it, ready to edit.

STEP 5

Once approved, keep an eye on parts.

01

Each part gets its own status — you'll see it update as the parts team processes it.

02

Once every part is either shipped or cancelled, a "Confirm" button appears on the claim.

03

Click it once the parts have physically arrived at your branch — this moves the claim forward.

If every part on a claim ends up cancelled, confirming will close the claim directly — no repair evidence needed, since there's nothing to repair.

STEP 6

Finish the repair, then submit your evidence.

01

Complete the repair using the parts that arrived.

02

Attach photos or a signed work order showing the completed repair.

03

Click "Submit After Repair." The dealer will review and close the claim — or send it back if something's off.

STEP 7

Every claim is saved, permanently.

Once a claim is closed, it moves to your History tab — searchable any time, with a complete record of every status change, edit, and note from start to finish. Nothing gets deleted or lost.
